## Gate A — make the core customer journey genuinely useful (engineering-owned)

1. **Source-to-profile accuracy (`P1-024/025/026`, `EC-003/006`).** For all ten supplied files and a 10,000-word document, inspect every original page; verify roles, employers, chronology, degrees, skills, achievements, links, and distinctions between awards/participation. AI must ask for ambiguous/missing details; the customer answers once before writing. Corrected facts must survive later transformations and regenerate dependent views. Reject omitted or shortened factual labels such as `B.E.` becoming `E.`. The sample corpus has structural passes, not full editorial approval.
2. **Expert review (`P1-033`, `P1-040–051`, `P3-030/031`).** Every review must quote exact source passages, have useful ranked changes, distinguish measured layout defects from speculative ATS concerns, assess writing, evidence, length, hierarchy, dates and visual presentation, and produce a polished PDF. Test real-source variation, stale AI caches, rate limits, fallback, and long-resume timeouts; prove successful expert reviews alone consume quota. Do not display internal score calibration.
3. **Resume/letter/LinkedIn creation (`P3-032–041`).** For each of the ten sources, compare the transformed output claim by claim with the original and customer corrections; review one complete export per style and stress scenario. The local builder now has saved customer-controlled section ordering, consistently rendered in PDF and DOCX, and a manual A4 visual check has no clipping or overlap. Resume-field undo, duplication, job tailoring in studio, useful writing hints, and deployed authenticated acceptance remain. Preserve resume/letter theme and factual labels. Cover letter and LinkedIn are separately requested and must work independently; no canned biography or padded letter. The four-style synthetic PDF matrix and one synthetic deployed journey are not enough.
   - 2026-09-26 local progress: transformation now fails semantic acceptance if a substantively documented source role is collapsed to empty or token bullets; sparse roles remain exempt. The full 296-test suite and typecheck pass. This prevents one low-value failure mode but does not prove expert writing quality or close this gate.
4. **Editor and dashboard (`P2-001–003`, founder screenshots).** Remove stretched/empty paired cards in the profile editor, keep long records readable and reachable, show genuine progress and clear calls to action, retain a compact chronological education-to-present roadmap with dated credentials/recognition, avoid zero-value profile signals and an overemphasized world map, and verify responsive/keyboard/mobile behavior. An authenticated visual review against real member data remains required.
5. **Goals and learning (`P4-001/002/009/010`).** Replace generic or repeated tasks with an AI plan based on the confirmed profile, target role/geography/date, actual gaps and vetted current courses. The local goal workspace now foregrounds the target context, progress, active areas, and editable actions; it must still prove that its plan is profile-specific, saves/reopens correctly, and is usable in browser/mobile review. Do not substitute a stock plan when AI silently fails. Verify saved/reopened/edited goals and course links.
6. **Salary benchmarking (`P4-011/012`, `EC-013`).** Name the feature correctly. Obtain public, source-cited role-and-region salary evidence, ideally segmented for seniority/experience and industry, then compute ranges and confidence transparently in local currency. The AI explains and compares sources; it cannot invent sources or market numbers. AI web research is now the primary candidate path, retaining only ranges tied to returned grounding URLs. Complete a browser E2E with a real non-sensitive benchmark, source-quality checks, sparse-data behavior and production cost/usage monitoring before launch.
7. **Jobs/recruiter (`P4-004/007/008`, `DM-004`, promoted `P7-006`).** Verify regional source credentials/coverage, 30-day freshness, daily feeds, duplicates/disappeared posts, salary fit, saved/dismissed/applied feedback, and first-party recruiter verification, postings, profile-only applications and messaging. Commercial boards require lawful partner access; no unsupported scraping. Google Jobs discovery is an auxiliary channel, not the sole feed.

3. Close the independent engineering/security/operations items in Gate B while the founder arranges Gate C approvals/credentials. Record attempts and evidence against the requirement IDs, not broad “done” statements.
4. Prove Razorpay test-mode, email, regional salary/jobs sources and isolated upload safety before enabling paid or broad real-customer acquisition.

Acceptance evidence for each gate must include code/tests, realistic authenticated staging behavior, adverse/fallback cases, desktop/mobile/accessibility review, source-grounded content or document inspection where applicable, deployment revision/digest, monitoring and rollback route. A local green test suite or one successful AI sample is not a launch approval.
